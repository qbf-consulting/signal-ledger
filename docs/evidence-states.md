# Claims and evidence states

Claims are derived propositions linked to an event. Their state is not inherited from source popularity.

The deterministic v0.3 evaluator follows three conservative rules:

1. challenging evidence yields `contradictory`;
2. absence of identified primary evidence yields `indeterminate`;
3. a claim is `verified` only when all identified primary evidence is also supporting evidence.

These states describe the ledger's evidence posture, not universal truth. Later freshness and authority policies may further constrain verification. Derived or AI-generated material cannot designate itself as primary evidence.
