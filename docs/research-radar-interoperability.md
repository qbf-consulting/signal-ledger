# Research Radar interoperability

Signal Ledger does not replace the Digital Governance Paper Notes Radar. It provides a provenance-preserving candidate contract that can be evaluated alongside the existing workflow.

The profile deliberately supports multiple discovery channels, including a random/exploratory sample. This prevents a known-author or known-topic catalogue from becoming a self-reinforcing boundary around the literature.

A research candidate exports its observation identifier, canonical URI, title, discovery channel, and evidence references. Editorial selection and review remain downstream decisions.

A future comparative run should measure recall, novelty, precision, duplication, disciplinary/geographic diversity, explainability, and conversion to published review before changing the existing Radar.
